NOVA CRYSTALS

Rebirth and Super Rebirth pay separately

Reaching RB35 through RB40 gives 300 Nova per regular rebirth. A Super Rebirth pays a separate amount based on the rank you have already completed. At RB40, that reset pays 529 Nova.

The green strip on each card shows the normal rebirth award. The orange strip shows the Super Rebirth payout after completing that rank. If your menu is asking for Rank 40 and you are still at RB39, your current Super Rebirth payout is 497, not 529.

HOW TO READ EACH CARD

01

Build the three droids

Each rebirth card lists the exact three droid targets for that cycle and rank.

02

Match variant and rarity

The required variant and droid rarity appear directly beneath every droid name.

03

Keep or sell

The right badge shows the droid's next use. SELL means it is not needed again in that cycle.

04

Read the two Nova payouts

Green shows Nova earned by reaching the rank. Orange shows the separate Super Rebirth Nova and credit/XP bonuses after completing that rank.
