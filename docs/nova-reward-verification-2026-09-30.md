# Nova rewards checked September 30, 2026

The chart previously displayed the separate Super Rebirth payout under a bare NOVA label. It did not display the smaller regular rebirth award. This was a presentation error, not evidence of a reward reduction.

Sources checked:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/StarWarsDroidTycoon/comments/1vtgnoy/super_rebirth_full_requirement_guide_with/ lists ordinary RB20-35 awards in its updated patch 1.30 section.
- https://www.reddit.com/r/StarWarsDroidTycoon/comments/1weirhf/number_of_crystals_you_get_for_rb_not_srb/ distinguishes RB from SRB.
- https://droidex.dubtrackr.win/updates/droid-tycoon-v1-32/ records 300 ordinary Nova for each RB36-40.
- https://droidex.dubtrackr.win/guides/droid-tycoon-super-rebirth-nova-crystals/ independently lists the complete SRB table, RB12-40, matching our existing values (RB35 379; RB36 407; RB37 436; RB38 466; RB39 497; RB40 529).
- https://droidarchives.co.uk/data/nova-shop.json fetched again today agrees with both tables. Its note distinguishes completed rank from the next rank displayed in the game menu.

No substantiated reference to a reduction of these rebirth payouts was found in the search. Other reported nerfs concern crate drops, chip rewards or shop prices. These remain community references rather than a direct in-game observation of Josh's current lobby.

The website now shows regular awards in a green card strip, explicitly labels the separate SRB payout and credit/XP bonuses in orange, and includes a complete RB12-40 table with source links. A Rank 40 target while still at completed RB39 means the current SRB offer is 497, not 529. The full run to RB40 earns 2,975 ordinary Nova plus 529 for the reset, totaling 3,504 before spending or unrelated rewards.
